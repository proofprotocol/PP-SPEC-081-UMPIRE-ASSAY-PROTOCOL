# AI Disclosure

*How this specification was made, and by whom.*

## Summary

Reproof is Craig Ellrod's idea. Claude helped with the research and drafted the words.

Craig Ellrod originated reproof, an independent party re-proving a proof, published the reproducibility requirement in the Proof Economy Prior Art Declaration (2026-07-10, Category 4a), and directed the research into whether Proof Protocol already specified it. Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, carried out that research at his direction, found that PP-SPEC-077 to 079 define record verification but not reproof, and drafted this specification under his direction. Claude was not the instigator of the idea or of the authorship.
